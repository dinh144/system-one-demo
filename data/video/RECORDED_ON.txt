Recorded on: ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 9 (Intel i7-1185G7, CPU only, no GPU), 2026-10-07.
Real run of the 10-turn scenario on the real backend (engines run one after another), driven in Chrome with Claude in Chrome and captured with its GIF recorder, then converted to H.264 mp4.
The recorder captures one frame per action, so this is a slideshow of 16 frames taken about 4 seconds apart while the scenario ran, each shown for 4 seconds. It is not a real-time screen capture, and the scenario itself ran faster than the video.
The latencies in the frames are real measurements from this laptop, with uncontrolled background load. Not recorded on the presenter machine.
